Lantern Thief — Shadow of the Ember Museum: a top-down stealth game. Slip past patrolling watchmen with sweeping vision cones, hide behind crates, pocket every ember gem, and reach the green exit unseen. Guards grow suspicious, investigate noises and near misses, and give chase when they spot you — break line of sight to cool them down. Toss pebbles to pull a guard away, sneak quietly past, and listen for footsteps. Three museum wings, three lives, one lantern.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# then open http://localhost:8080
```

Controls: WASD / arrows — move · SHIFT — sneak (slow, silent) · CLICK — toss a pebble to distract a guard · P — pause · M — mute.
